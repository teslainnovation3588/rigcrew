CAREER PREPARATION

BE READY FOR
THE ENERGY INDUSTRY.

Understand common qualifications, safety training and professional experience required across oil and gas operations.

Requirements differ by employer, country, position and type of operation. The information below is a general guide, not a guarantee of eligibility for any particular vacancy.

🎓

Education & Qualifications

Employers may request qualifications relevant to the position.

  • Technical certificates or diplomas
  • Engineering or science degrees
  • Trade qualifications
  • Role-specific professional credentials
🛢️

Industry Experience

Experience requirements depend on the seniority and responsibilities of the role.

  • Entry-level or graduate experience
  • Previous industrial employment
  • Offshore or onshore experience
  • Equipment and operational knowledge
🦺

Safety Training

Some positions require recognised safety training.

  • BOSIET or applicable offshore safety training
  • HUET where required
  • HSE awareness
  • Emergency response training
🏥

Medical Fitness

Some employers require occupational medical assessments.

  • Employer-approved medical examination
  • Offshore medical certification where applicable
  • Fitness for assigned duties
📄

Professional Documents

Depending on the position, employers may request:

  • Updated professional CV
  • Academic or trade certificates
  • Employment references
  • Valid identification and travel documents
🌍

International Readiness

International assignments may have additional conditions.

  • Right to work in the destination country
  • Applicable immigration authorisation
  • Employer-specific onboarding
  • Language and communication skills

Important Information

Never assume that a training certificate guarantees employment. Certification requirements must be confirmed against the actual vacancy and employer. Visa, work permit and medical requirements must be verified with the relevant official authorities and authorised providers. Do not pay individuals promising guaranteed jobs or visas.
