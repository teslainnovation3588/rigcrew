Privacy Policy

Information about privacy and the handling of personal information on RIGCREW.

Current website status: RIGCREW is currently a website prototype. The contact form is not connected to a submission service, and job applications, document uploads and applicant payments are not currently enabled.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how RIGCREW intends to handle personal information when its website services become operational. We aim to respect privacy and handle information responsibly and transparently.

2. Information We May Collect

If application and contact services are activated in the future, information may include:

  • Name and contact details.
  • Professional experience and employment history.
  • Qualifications and relevant certifications.
  • Information submitted through an enquiry or application form.

Only information necessary for a clearly explained purpose should be requested.

3. How Information May Be Used

Subject to applicable law and the relevant service being activated, information may be used to:

  • Respond to enquiries.
  • Process applications for verified vacancies.
  • Communicate relevant recruitment information.
  • Maintain platform security and prevent misuse.

4. Sharing Information

Personal information should only be shared with relevant employers, properly authorised recruitment partners or service providers where there is a lawful basis and appropriate transparency. Information must not be sold or disclosed for unrelated purposes.

5. Data Security

When operational, reasonable technical and organisational safeguards will be used to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss or misuse.

6. Data Retention

Information should only be retained for as long as necessary for its stated purpose or as required by applicable law.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, individuals may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of or restrictions on the use of their personal information.

8. International Users

Because RIGCREW is intended for an international audience, applicable privacy requirements may differ depending on the country where an individual lives or works.

9. Contact

For privacy enquiries, please use the RIGCREW contact page once its communication service has been activated.

10. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated as RIGCREW develops. The effective date and material changes should be published when the operational service launches.
