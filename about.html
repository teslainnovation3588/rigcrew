ABOUT RIGCREW

CONNECTING PEOPLE
WITH ENERGY CAREERS.

RIGCREW is being developed as a global oil and gas career information platform, designed to help professionals explore opportunities and understand the requirements of the energy industry.

Who We Are

The energy industry depends on skilled, dedicated and qualified professionals. RIGCREW aims to make career information easier to discover by bringing relevant oil and gas career resources together in one accessible platform.


Our platform is being developed to serve professionals interested in offshore and onshore operations, engineering, drilling, maintenance, health and safety, logistics and other energy-sector career paths.

🎯

Our Mission

To make oil and gas career information more accessible and help professionals understand available career paths, qualifications and employer requirements.

🌍

Our Vision

To develop a trusted global platform for discovering energy-sector career information and connecting professionals with legitimate industry opportunities.

What We Stand For

Transparency

Clear information about vacancies, requirements and recruitment processes.

Professionalism

Respect for the skills, experience and contributions of energy professionals.

Verification

Commitment to checking employer authority and vacancy information before publication.
